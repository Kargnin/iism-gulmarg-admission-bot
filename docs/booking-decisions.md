# Booking behavior and evidence

Each preference has a primary number and its own worker. Extra numbers are exclusively leased reserves for explicitly used/persistently refused primaries. Every preference is attempted. Missing/full/disappeared schedules remain pending until booked or cancelled.

Course names remain flexible. Configuration uses unambiguous whole-word course-kind/level semantics and rejects incompatible combinations before requests. The cascade is course → level → schedule → state → DOB, carrying each response's hidden fields forward. Optional text hints break ties only within the same semantic rank; they neither move generic choices ahead of stronger evidence, block renamed options, override contradictory semantic/date evidence, nor define identity. The cascade keeps exploring compatible courses and levels when an earlier matching option has no seats. A full verdict at course, level or schedule selection starts a fresh mobile/WebForms visit while retaining only that scan's candidate progress; the next polling round retries every candidate. Combined course/level/batch evidence rejects explicit competing course kinds or skiing levels while retaining the observed generic Snow Skiing parent with the requested non-skiing leaf and genuinely generic date-led options. Parsed date-token spans enforce every explicitly written start/end pair without counting two interpretations of one numeric token twice. When no known evidence conflicts but a generic course/level is supported only by aligned dates, the intentionally permissive request proceeds with a runtime warning. A visible course does not prove that its schedules are open. When at least two entries of a schedule list carry written years matching this season's dates, a lone written year that disagrees with a target's day and month in one entry is treated as the site's typo rather than another season's batch. Each visit starts with a fresh cookie jar, because the course list refreshes only that way: with bot.directForm by opening admission_form.aspx?mobile=N straight away, which the live site served to a new session on 12 Nov 2026 and whose forms it answered "Seat Not Available" on 13 Nov (Kargnin's shortcut: one request instead of five), otherwise at mobile verification; if the site does not serve the form that way the bot goes back to mobile verification for the rest of the run. The rush is one minute before bookingOpensAt until 60 minutes after it, and also the 60 minutes after this season's dates come back on the site having been missing for at least 30 s (since 13 Nov the site lists courses without dates between releases; on 12 Nov the dates came with the seats). During the rush every pause is kept as short as possible (step retries about 0.1 s, held-form resends about 0.1 s, unlisted choices looked for every second, fresh visits after at most 2 s), like Kargnin's direct-POST loop; outside it the bot looks every 30 s and resends a held form every 30 s. Within a visit, a server error on one step (busy 429/502/503/504, a dropped or timed-out connection) resends that same request with the same cookies and page state for up to stepRetrySec, while the site's own error page (HTTP 500, an unexpected page, an invalid seat count) is resent at most three times; a lost session, a rejected page state or an exhausted step starts a fresh visit. A listed choice whose seats are 0 is reported as having no seats, not as unlisted. With holdForm (default) such a choice is still filled in through state and date of birth, and its final form is sent and resent: the site checks seats when the final form arrives (Kargnin's direct-POST observation, 12 Nov 2026), so a held form books with one request once seats open. A "no seats" or closed answer to the final form keeps the filled form. A FormRefresher fills it in afresh on a separate session in the background - once five minutes before opening, every 2 minutes during the rush after opening, every 15 minutes after the rush - while the held form keeps being sent; the fresh form replaces it only once complete and ready to send, so a form filled in before opening is never dropped even if the site empties its course list again, and a held form whose hidden values went stale is replaced.

Certainty is decided at the original final POST boundary. A reply that has arrived is judged even while stopping (13 Nov 2026: Ctrl+C during the rush otherwise left five final forms the site had answered "Seat Not Available" unresolved); stopping only prevents new requests; the redirect and receipt of a final form already sent are still followed and loaded, so its outcome is settled. With bot.maxBookings N the run stops once N bookings are confirmed (dry runs ignore it), and while a choice's final form has been redirected and its receipt is being confirmed the other choices hold their next final form for up to 60 s - in a rehearsal of the 17 Nov reopening at 95% IIS 503.2, up to five bookings otherwise landed while the first receipt loaded. Unresolved final forms saved by an earlier run are asked about at startup, before waiting for the opening time, so no choice is held unanswered through the rush. A connection is given CONNECT_TIMEOUT_SEC (5 s, TLS included) to open, then the request its full timeout; a connection that does not open never carried the form and is tried afresh. A per-attempt marker changes only immediately before the first HTTP request bytes may be written: DNS, TCP or TLS failures before that point clear the unused pending record, while partial writes, in-flight requests, redirects and receipt failures preserve it for feedback. Cancellation is rechecked at that same write boundary, and one absolute request deadline covers status lines, every redirect-body drain, headers and final bodies. Malformed, credential-bearing and cross-origin redirects are blocked before contact; after a final write they become uncertainty only for that preference, while pre-send destination errors remain configuration failures. A declared Content-Length must be fully consumed; early EOF and timed-out bodies are incomplete for every HTTP status, while valid zero-body, lengthless EOF-framed and chunked responses retain their protocol semantics. A complete pure direct 429, known used/full/closed response or actual failed-validator state proves rejection; an incomplete response is never treated as that evidence. A direct 429, or a reply Windows/IIS itself sends before the site's code runs - IIS's 503.2 page (concurrent request limit), HTTP.sys's own "Service Unavailable" page (Server: Microsoft-HTTPAPI; queue full or site restarting), an IIS detailed error raised before any handler was chosen ("Handler: Not yet determined") or IIS Dynamic IP Restrictions (403.501-403.504) - resends the same final form; any other 503 or error may come from a proxy or from code that already processed the form, and stays uncertain. When the final POST redirected and only the receipt page then failed with a server error, that receipt URL alone is loaded again by GET (the form is never resent) and judged as a redirected response, so only a clear receipt confirms it. A connection failure while the request is still being written (before any redirect) means the site never received the whole form, so it is resent; a failure after the last byte was written stays uncertain. Affirmative success must full-match the entire normalized message envelope as a known completed positive submission construction, optionally followed only by the observed payment-email receipt suffix. Question/semicolon punctuation and adjacent qualifiers are preserved rather than split away. ASCII/curly contractions, negating determiners, cannot-confirm or verify/check instructions, quoted prose, prefix/suffix conditions, future/hypothetical wording, invalid, rejected, not-accepted and generic receipt wording are not proof. Conflict detection separately recognizes supported completion references that are not a complete known negative-only, instructional, future or conditional outcome; a prior rejection/error, changed subject, pronoun, omitted subject, punctuation or conjunction cannot turn mixed evidence into a safe replay. If genuine success appears together with HTTP 429, used/full/closed, failure wording or a failed validator, the result is conflicting regardless of HTTP status: durable pending state remains and only that batch waits for feedback. An unknown label is likewise ambiguous even on a direct 500. A final form answered directly (no redirect) with a page-state rejection - viewstate MAC, event validation or invalid state - was not processed, because ASP.NET checks those before any of the page's own code runs (for example after an app restart invalidated a held form); its marker is cleared and the form filled in afresh. Accepted records store canonical location/date/kind/level identity under an explicit key version. Trusted unmatched history stays visible and protects its phone while a changed kind/level may use a reserve; older or unversioned progress fails closed without migration or date-based guessing.

Inputs and files are prepared once before workers start; uploads are shared immutable snapshots. Config, schedule, rules and progress decoding reject duplicate object keys rather than normalizing them. Progress uses process locking and durable atomic writes, rejects reversed ranges, stores canonical semantic identity and phone strings, and validates every persisted summary/receipt field it consumes. Read-only check and booking validate the same applicant/season/origin identity. Existing and unresolved applications are never silently discarded or normalized. Bot-owned run directories are owner-only on POSIX, as are logs, state/lock files, saved responses and converted document copies; original configured documents are not chmodded or rewritten.

Schedule identity uses a required canonical adjacent-year `YYYY-YY` financial season. Effective source, top-level override and preference dates must remain within 1 April through 31 March of that declared season; a new financial year requires an explicitly relabeled replacement schedule and therefore a distinct run and progress identity. Each configured date field contains exactly one written date span, while live dropdown text may contain the course range. Opening-time overrides remain independent.

Applicant state, education and diet values resolve once at startup: exact website text and declared state aliases rank first, followed by a unique actual-option acronym or plausible spelling, spacing or abbreviation match. Ambiguous initials are reported with candidate labels. The resolved website labels are shown before booking and are matched again against the current live options. Missing or unresolvable essentials remain actionable errors. Shared non-sending preparation validates the latest form for both dry-run and booking: it must still select the intended course, level, batch and state, those IDs must carry compatible text/meaning, the form must echo the configured DOB and exact assigned canonical mobile, and mandatory enabled controls plus the exact submit button must remain present. Seats are complete nonnegative integers. The current website fee must be a positive numeric readiness value, but it is never estimated or compared with a client expectation; the browser value is carried unchanged for manual payment through the later email link. Required WebForms/capacity tokens must be nonblank, and the capacity allocation ID must not be its zero placeholder. Opaque current token values are carried unchanged; blank event target/argument fields remain valid for the final button submit. Dry-run stops after that preparation without durable submission state or a final request. Every admission-form postback also requires the assigned mobile echo before carrying further form fields; mobile entry and criteria remain supported without that control. Optional controls follow browser submission rules. Explicit website age below 18 requires the real guardian file/control. A guardian validator can require a configured real file and a fresh visit even when numeric age was absent; once required, no final request starts until that file control returns. Missing website age remains unknown and never enables applicant-signature substitution. That authorized substitution is available only after the website positively reports adulthood and still insists. An absent, reset or empty mandatory control restarts from a fresh mobile session. Criteria-page refusals surface throttled actionable reason text while retaining the same phone and indefinitely retrying the preference from a fresh visit; only persistent mobile-number refusal uses a reserve. Unexpected worker programming failures stop sibling polling before best-effort error logging and produce a failing process result even if logging or a read-only confirmation prompt remains blocked; late feedback cannot alter saved progress after shutdown.

Persisted semantic dates use canonical `YYYY-MM-DD` text only. A target cannot simultaneously be recorded as accepted and assigned or pending submission; malformed current-version state is preserved and rejected before requests.

Certificate-verification fallback and optional adult applicant-signature reuse when the site insists on a guardian upload are intentional retained behaviors. Runtime guardian classification uses only the website-reported age or its explicit guardian validator; no local DOB/course-date inference is used. Reported minors require a real guardian signature and are held before final submission when it is missing. Progress has no reset/archive mode and must be preserved and reconciled. Intermediate/Advance entries receive an unsupported-path warning; no full live support is claimed.

## Observed on 2 November 2026

- The [admission flow](https://admission.iismgulmarg.in/Admission_instructions.aspx) is mobile entry → criteria → multipart admission_form.aspx.
- It uses ASP.NET WebForms / .NET 4.8, not PHP. Course, course level, schedule, state and DOB invoke __doPostBack and reload the form at the same URL. VIEWSTATE, VIEWSTATEGENERATOR, EVENTVALIDATION and other hidden inputs must come from the current response.
- Gulmarg course: **Snow Skiing Courses at Gulmarg**; levels: **Basic SSC**, **Intermediate SSC**, **Advance SSC**, **Snow Boarding**. Kargil includes **Basic skiing course**; MOI is separate. These observations inform tests; IDs are not hardcoded.
- Course/level lists were populated while schedule lists remained empty, including after selecting Basic. Real schedule IDs, allocation values and calculated fees remain unknown until schedules appear.
- The form has 94 controls and 35 validators, including hdfTypeOfCapacitySheet and hdfCapacitySheetAllocationId. Live values are carried through rather than fabricated.
- Current state, education, diet and maxlength rules are in form-rules.json. The fuller sanitized snapshot in tests/fixtures/live-form-2026-11-02.json excludes applicant data, phone numbers, documents and session tokens.
- Published instructions and visible upload labels require JPEG/JPG/PNG up to 500 KB; some script validators mention 1 MB/PDF/GIF. Prepared uploads use the stricter published requirement.
- Normal Chrome interaction produced a viewstate-MAC failure during a state postback. Availability checks also saw IIS 500 (missing PleskModule). These are server/page-state errors, not evidence that a course is full.

## Observed on 12 November 2026 (booking day)

- The course list was empty until 10:00. From 10:00 to about 10:20 most requests got IIS "503.2 Service Unavailable" (concurrent request limit), plus some connection resets and refusals. Visits that restarted at mobile verification after every error never reached a seat count, while people resending the failing step by hand saw 5 to 27 seats. By 10:20 every Gulmarg schedule showed 0 seats.
- Gulmarg schedules (the same list under every level, Snow Boarding included): "1st course from 20 Dec 2026 to 02 Jan 2027", "2nd course from 03rd Jan to 16 Jan 2027", "3rd course from 17 Jan to 30 Jan 2027", "4th course from 31 Jan to 13 Feb 2026" (the site's year typo for 2027), "5th course from 14 Feb to 27 Feb 2027", "7th course from 28 Feb to 13 Mar 2027".
- Kargil levels "Basic skiing course", "Intermediate skiing course", "Advance skiing course"; schedules "1st course from 8th Feb to 21 Feb 2027", "2nd course from 22 Feb to 07 Mar 2027". MOI Courses at Gulmarg: level "None", schedule "6th course from 14 Feb to 27 Feb 2027".
- Besides IIS's 503.2 page, the bot met HTTP.sys's own "Service Unavailable" page 5 times (and connection resets and refusals). HTTP.sys sends that page only for a request it never handed to the site.
- In the evening, `admission_form.aspx?mobile=N` opened the admission form, with the number echoed and the course list, for a brand-new session (no mobile verification or terms), as Kargnin's direct-POST script assumes; a dry run of all seven choices through that path passed every pre-submit check. The G4 schedule label then read "... to 13 Feb 2027" (the typo was fixed).
- With 0 seats every Gulmarg schedule can still be filled in through state and date of birth, and the form then carries a positive fee and its capacity fields. G1 carries hdfTypeOfCapacitySheet "ScheduleCourseWise" with hdfCapacitySheetAllocationId "0", a real value rather than a reset placeholder. A final POST of such a form while full was answered "Seat Not Available" (Kargnin's manual test).

The [official 2026–27 schedule](https://www.iismgulmarg.in/Courses_By_IISM.html#snowski2026) confirms the stored ranges and opening on 12 November 2026 at 10:00. A [January 2025 firsthand walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vIy0eV-R94M) corroborates the general flow and 500 KB preparation advice; old values are not current-season evidence.

## Observed on 13 November 2026

- No seats were released. From 09:40 every final form was answered "Seat Not Available"; at 10:00 some new connections hung for ~25 s (Errno 60) while replies kept coming at about one per second per choice.
- By 12:30 the Gulmarg schedules were gone again: course and levels listed, no dates under any level - as before the 12 Nov opening.

## Observed on 16 November 2026

- IISM announced (Instagram) that online booking reopens on 17 November 2026 at 10:00 AM for a few seats vacated "due to non payment on time and multiple entries by some students"; first come, first served; payment within 12 hours of booking.
- At 17:33 the admission form listed no courses at all, as before the 12 Nov opening.

## Observed on 17 November 2026 (reopening)

- The reopening was about 10 minutes late. The course list stayed empty until 10:07, then listed the Gulmarg course and its levels with no dates. From 10:10 the batches appeared one at a time (G1, then G2, G3, G4, G5 and G6 by 10:14), and every listed batch already showed its seats.
- All of them were gone by 10:34. Snowboarding was offered in G1 and G4 only; in G2, G3, G5 and G6 the Snow Boarding level leaves `hdfCapacitySheetAllocationId` empty, so no final form can be built.
- The success receipt is `AdmissionFormSuccess.aspx?Resp=Admission Form Submitted Successfully.`, reached by a redirect from the final POST, with the label "Admission Form submitted successfully, Payment details have been sent on your registered email. Please finish the payment process within 12 hours.!". The bot now accepts that wording; before, it held the booking as uncertain.
- Several final forms came back as the unchanged form with only "Please change the mobile number." and `hdfAdmissionFormId` 0, so no application was created. A form opened the same way with another number booked at the same time, so the shortcut is not the cause. The site refuses those numbers, and the bot now treats the message as a used number and moves on to a reserve.
- The server saves uploads as a timestamp to the second plus the uploaded file name. Two final forms uploading the same file name in the same second crashed one of them with HTTP 500 ("The process cannot access the file ... because it is being used by another process"). The bot judges that reply as uncertain.

## Unverified boundaries

No real application or payment was submitted. A historical response supports accepting explicit successful-submission messaging without an application ID; exact current production success/duplicate/full responses remain unverified. Mock outcomes test possibilities and do not prove live behavior.

Preflight checks presence/type/size rather than identity or legibility. The institute's one-course-per-person-per-year and payment rules apply. Tests and retries cannot guarantee admission.
